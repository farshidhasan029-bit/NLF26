ABOUT THE FESTIVAL

National Language Fest (NLF '26) brings together students from universities and colleges across Bangladesh to celebrate linguistic heritage, competitive oratory, and literature. Organized by Bangladesh Agricultural University Language Club (BAULC) on BAU's historic 1,200-acre green campus in Mymensingh.

FLAGSHIP SEGMENTS

Six competitive tracks open for institutional delegations and individual entries.

Track 01 • Bangla

Bangla Shahitto & Olympiad

Written examination covering classical Bengali literature, grammar, etymology, and language history.

Track 02 • English

National Spelling Bee

Fast-paced stage contest challenging participants with complex root origins, phonetics, and advanced lexicons.

Track 03 • Bilingual

Extempore Speech & Oratory

Spontaneous public speaking on contemporary socio-cultural topics with 3 minutes of preparation.

Track 04 • Creative

Creative Writing & Recitation

Original poetry and prose composition alongside emotive stage recitation celebrating linguistic beauty.

Track 05 • Team Track

Linguistics & Literature Quiz

Interactive team quiz testing breadth of world literature, classical mythology, and linguistic geography.

Track 06 • Open Track

Translation Challenge

Nuanced contextual translation between Bangla, English, and local idioms preserving tone and rhythm.
