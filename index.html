History • Heritage • Impact

LEGACY OF NLF

From its inception in 2018 to nationwide editions, explore the journey of BAULC and the National Language Fest.

MEET THE HOST

Prothom Alo Feature • 21/09/2025 বাংলাদেশ কৃষি বিশ্ববিদ্যালয় ল্যাঙ্গুয়েজ ক্লাব • National Media

Bangladesh Agricultural University Language Club (BAULC) is a non-profit organization that embarked on its journey in 2018. The club is guided by the motto of exploring the world of language and is dedicated to nurturing the communication and linguistic proficiencies of students within the BAU campus. In pursuit of this goal, BAULC regularly organizes monthly presentation events on diverse subjects, providing students with opportunities to cultivate their presentation skills. Furthermore, it hosts workshops & sessions on various topics, aiding students in refining their soft skills. The club also coordinates an array of language based competitions among the student body. With an Executive Committee comprising more than 60 members, BAULC is resolutely committed to preparing students for a brighter future and equipping them to confront the forthcoming challenges with confidence.

NLF23

Inaugural Edition • 2023
National Language Fest 2023 • Grand Gala BAU Central Auditorium Stage

The National Language Fest 2023 hosted by the Bangladesh Agricultural University Language Club, was a groundbreaking two-day celebration of languages and cultures. Organized for the very first time, it marked a historic moment as Bangladesh Agricultural University witnessed such a unique and dynamic linguistic festival on its campus. Open to students nationwide, the event featured language-based competitions and insightful sessions conducted by expert speakers. Participants engaged in script writing, cultural performances, vocabulary challenges and linguistic explorations across Bangla, English, Japanese, French and Nepali. The second day offered practical skill-building workshops on IELTS, public speaking and email writing, culminating in a grand prize-giving ceremony that celebrated talent, creativity, and the power of expression.

NLF24

BRAC Bank Presents • 2024
BRAC Bank Presents NLF 2024 • Grand Stage BAU Central Auditorium

BRAC Bank Presents National Language Fest 2024 organized by the Bangladesh Agricultural University Language Club was an even more vibrant and impactful celebration following the success of its debut edition in 2023. The fest featured an expanded range of intellectual and cultural competitions designed to celebrate the beauty and power of language through creativity, communication and critical thinking. Esteemed speakers, language professionals and industry experts conducted engaging sessions that enriched participants with practical knowledge and inspiration. With stronger nationwide reach, enhanced event design and amplified youth engagement, BRAC Bank Presents National Language Fest 2024 became a remarkable platform where passion met purpose and language built connections beyond boundaries.
